# One-Time Task Reminders

Implemented ordinary task-linked self-reminders: protected local-time/IANA preview, explicit DST choice, atomic durable creation, current admission/task/consent checks, cancellation and bounded independent dispatch. The worker creates one uniquely bound in-app notification and scheduler-attributed event in the same transaction. Pending work is recovered from PostgreSQL, not a client timer or model session.

Recipient-approved requests now let a current task manager propose an exact time to the current eligible assignee. A proposal creates no scheduled work or notification. Only the recipient's protected review/acceptance atomically creates their personal reminder and required audit/outbox records. Read-time expiry, immutable retries, exact admission checks, preference generations, decline/withdrawal and acceptance races are covered by PostgreSQL tests. Requesters see the proposal outcome but not the recipient's private schedule ID/read/acknowledgment.

Self endpoints remain self-only. Acceptance creates independently recipient-owned work, not standing permission for the requester. Escalation, arbitrary/standing third-party consent, quiet-hour alerts, care records and external providers remain retained but unimplemented. A due date never implies a reminder.

Repeating reminders ([DEC-010](../../../../docs/DECISIONS.md#accepted-decisions), provisional): `recurrence.py` computes daily and weekly rules with `zoneinfo`; a skipped clock time moves just past the change or is skipped, as the person chose, and a repeated one takes the first. `series.py` stores the rule in `reminder_series` and keeps only the next time as a reminder (`series_id`, `occurrence_date`, with a key derived from the series and the date). The worker's delivery continues the series in the same transaction and, after downtime, delivers at most one late time. Pause, resume, skip and cancel require `Idempotency-Key` and `If-Match` and keep receipts in `reminder_series_commands`; changes go to `reminder_series_events` and the outbox. Changing or closing the task pauses a series; losing access, the account or the preference suppresses it. A series time cannot be cancelled as a plain reminder (409 `USE_SERIES_ACTIONS`). Tests: `backend/tests/test_reminder_series.py`.
