# Decisions

The register of project decisions. The product owner makes product decisions. Architecture decisions are written as ADRs in [adr/](adr/) and listed here. Each contract also keeps its own decision table, with IDs such as C3-D01; those stay in the contract and are summarized below.

## How Decisions Are Recorded

- Only the product owner approves a decision. An AI assistant can prepare one but never approve it.
- A decision that changes an approved requirement names the requirement and gives the old and new wording. Nothing else changes an approved requirement.
- Conflicts are handled as [Article 5 of the Product Constitution](PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md#article-5-resolving-conflicts) describes. A decision takes the level of the document it changes, and is written into that document before anything is implemented from it.
- Decisions are never deleted. A later decision replaces an earlier one and says so.

Template:

```text
### DEC-NNN Title
- Date:
- Decided by:
- Decision:
- Changes: requirement or decision replaced, with old and new wording
- Reason:
- Documents updated:
```
